What Is to Be Done About Punxsutawney Phil?

Phil is not unstoppable no matter how prolific his name is in North America.

Elixir of Life

Phil has been drinking an elixir to extend his life for years now. Every sip extends his life by 7 years.

According to Christopher Klein of History.com, The elixir, made of vodka, milk and orange juice, was said to add seven years to Phil’s life.

Alternative Sources of Information

Phil has been spiraling and his handlers know it. On the official Groundhog Day Website, the predictive power that Phil has is little more than a consensus among other celebrated creatures both taxidermied and living that either Winter is longer or Spring is coming.

List of Other Known Sources of Predictive Knowledge

Potomac Phil
Washington, D.C.
Potomac Phil is a taxidermied groundhog and has shown that even without a currently beating heart, predictions come easy to those with the ability to make them. He is currently on the look for an elixir of life of his own.
Walnut the Hedgehog
Dayton, Ohio
Walnut is a newcomer to the prediction market and has so far only made four predictions. Time will only tell if this rodent can predict the weather or become a charlatan like Phil is.
